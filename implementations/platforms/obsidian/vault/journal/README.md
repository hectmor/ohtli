# Journal

## Purpose

The Journal records work as it happens.

Entries are organized chronologically.

## What belongs here

- Daily notes (`daily/`, managed by the Obsidian Daily Notes plugin)
- Weekly notes
- Journal entries (`entries/`)
- Observations

Meetings are their own Domain Object and live in `meetings/`, not here.

## Workflow

Journal entries may generate:

- Projects
- Knowledge notes
- Tasks

The Journal is a historical record rather than permanent knowledge.
