# Workspace

The workspace is the operational environment of Ohtli.

Each directory has a single responsibility and represents a distinct domain within the framework.

## Inbox

Temporary location for captured information.

## Areas

Long-term responsibilities that require continuous maintenance.

## Projects

Temporary efforts with a defined objective.

## Knowledge

Reference material intended for future use.

## Journal

Chronological records of work and observations.

## Reviews

Periodic evaluations of the workspace.

## Templates

Reusable document templates.

## Attachments

Supporting files such as images, PDFs, or datasets.

## Archive

Inactive material retained for historical purposes.

The workspace is intentionally simple, tool-independent, and relies on the filesystem rather than platform-specific features.
