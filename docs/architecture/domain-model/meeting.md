# Meeting

## Purpose

Capture the outcome of a conversation.

## Identity

A meeting represents a specific interaction occurring at a particular
time.

## Responsibilities

- Record participants.
- Record decisions.
- Record action items.

## Essential Attributes

- Date
- Participants
- Outcome

## Lifecycle

- Recorded

Contextual presence (Operational vs. Historical) is a separate
dimension, independent from Lifecycle, defined by the Archive Workflow
(`Lifecycle Independence`, `archive-workflow.md`). It is not a
Lifecycle value.
