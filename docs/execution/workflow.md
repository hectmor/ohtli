# Workflow

Every piece of work follows the same execution workflow.

```text
Capture
    ↓
Clarify
    ↓
Plan
    ↓
Execute
    ↓
Review
    ↓
Archive
```

## Capture

Collect information without evaluating it.

## Clarify

Determine what the information represents and what action, if any, is required.

## Plan

Organize work and establish priorities.

## Execute

Perform the planned work.

## Review

Evaluate outcomes, capture lessons learned, and identify improvements.

## Archive

Move completed or inactive information out of the active workspace while preserving its history.
