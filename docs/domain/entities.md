# Domain Entities

This document defines the core entities of the Ohtli framework.

## Inbox

A temporary location for unprocessed information.

Everything enters the system through the Inbox.

---

## Project

A temporary effort undertaken to achieve a specific outcome.

Projects have a defined objective and eventually reach completion or are abandoned.

---

## Area

A long-term responsibility that requires continuous attention.

Areas do not have an end date.

---

## Knowledge

Reusable information that supports understanding, decision-making, or execution.

Knowledge is independent of any specific project.

---

## Journal

A chronological record of events, observations, progress, or decisions.

Journals provide historical context.

---

## Review

A structured evaluation of work, knowledge, or responsibilities.

Reviews ensure continuous improvement.

---

## Template

A reusable document that standardizes information capture.

Templates improve consistency and reduce repetitive work.

---

## Attachment

A non-Markdown resource associated with another entity.

Examples include images, PDFs, spreadsheets, and other supporting files.

---

## Dashboard

A view that summarizes information from multiple entities.

Dashboards never own data.

They only present it.
