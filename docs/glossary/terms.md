# Terms

## Area

A long-term responsibility without a defined end date.

---

## Attachment

A non-Markdown resource associated with another entity.

---

## Automation

A mechanism that may initiate a Workflow Execution automatically, without requiring a manual alternative. See the [Automation Model](../architecture/automation-model/README.md).

---

## Dashboard

A view that summarizes information from multiple entities without owning the underlying data.

---

## Domain Model

The conceptual representation of the entities managed by Ohtli and the relationships between them.

---

## Entity

Historical term for what the canonical architecture calls a Domain
Object. See the [Domain Model](../architecture/domain-model/README.md).

---

## Filesystem

The physical organization of information on disk. See the
[Filesystem Model](../architecture/filesystem-model/README.md).

---

## Inbox

Historical term from early ("M1") documentation for an unprocessed-item
holding area. It is not a Domain Object in the canonical
[Domain Model](../architecture/domain-model/README.md).

---

## Journal

A chronological record of events, observations, or decisions.

---

## Knowledge

Reusable information intended to support understanding or execution.

---

## Metadata

Structured information describing a document. See the
[Metadata Model](../architecture/metadata-model/README.md).

---

## Project

A temporary effort undertaken to achieve a specific outcome.

---

## Review

An evaluative transformation belonging to the
[Workflow Model](../architecture/workflow-model/README.md), not a Domain
Object.

---

## Template

A reusable document structure used to standardize information capture.

---

## View

A read-only representation of one or more entities.

---

## Workflow

A sequence of activities that transforms information into completed work.
