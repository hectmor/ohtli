# Terms

## Area

A long-term responsibility without a defined end date.

---

## Attachment

A non-Markdown resource associated with another entity.

---

## Automation

A process that assists users by reducing repetitive work while preserving the framework's principles.

---

## Dashboard

A view that summarizes information from multiple entities without owning the underlying data.

---

## Domain Model

The conceptual representation of the entities managed by Ohtli and the relationships between them.

---

## Entity

A core concept within the Ohtli domain.

---

## Filesystem

The physical organization of information on disk.

---

## Inbox

The entry point for unprocessed information.

---

## Journal

A chronological record of events, observations, or decisions.

---

## Knowledge

Reusable information intended to support understanding or execution.

---

## Metadata

Structured information describing a document.

---

## Project

A temporary effort undertaken to achieve a specific outcome.

---

## Review

A structured evaluation of work, responsibilities, or the framework itself.

---

## Template

A reusable document structure used to standardize information capture.

---

## View

A read-only representation of one or more entities.

---

## Workflow

A sequence of activities that transforms information into completed work.
