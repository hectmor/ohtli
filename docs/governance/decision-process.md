# Decision Process

Changes to Ohtli should follow a structured decision process.

## 1. Identify the Need

Clearly describe the problem or opportunity.

---

## 2. Evaluate Alternatives

Consider multiple approaches before selecting one.

Document significant trade-offs.

---

## 3. Record the Decision

Architectural decisions must be documented using an ADR.

Minor editorial changes do not require an ADR.

---

## 4. Implement

Apply the approved decision consistently across the framework.

---

## 5. Review

Evaluate whether the decision achieved its intended outcome.

Future ADRs may supersede previous decisions when justified.

## Principles

* Transparency
* Simplicity
* Consistency
* Traceability
* Long-term maintainability
